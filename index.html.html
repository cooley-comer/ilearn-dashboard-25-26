Verdict: Sophomores Outperformed Freshmen — But Both Grades Need Support

Sophomores fared better across every measured metric. They posted a higher average scale score (555 vs. 550.6), nearly double the At/Above Proficiency rate combined (38.1% vs. 20.9%), and more than twice the College & Career Readiness rate (38.1% vs. 20.8%). That said, both grades trail the Indiana state average of 560, and neither cohort reaches 50% proficiency. The most urgent intervention target is Heredity & Evolution (RC3) — the weakest reporting category for both cohorts. The large "Approaching Proficiency" cluster in 9th grade (41.7% — 30 students) represents the highest-leverage intervention opportunity: moving even half of that group to "At Proficiency" would dramatically shift the school's overall numbers next cycle.
